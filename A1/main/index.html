Why I like cheese:

There are so many reasons why I like cheese. Firstly, you can put cheese on mostly anything, other than maybe cereal, but you can put it on basically any type of bread, even some vegetables, pasta, basically most things that involve bread. I also love bread, which might be part of why I like cheese so much, but I would rather have cheese without bread over bread without cheese, that would just be weird. What even is cheese though but curdled milk, and then you also can have cheese curds, which isn't that just all cheese? Yes, but some are formed into a ball or a brick, while other forms of cheese stay as curds, such as the cheese curds used in poutine. There are also so many ways to enjoy cheese, some being smoked and having an amazing aftertaste, to being slightly salty, to being overly creamy, to being a string that you pull apart as a snack. Then there are things like a four-cheese blend for a pizza, where there is an unspecified combination of four different cheeses, and they always make you have to dig to find that it is four very similar-tasting cheeses. Cheese also is not always a solid, some cheeses like burrata have an unnecessary amount of cream inside them that it essentially bursts like a water balloon when cut open, then there are cheeses that are liquid like queso and other cheese dips, but those typically are just a form of cheese, rather than an entirely different cheese. One of the biggest issues about cheese for me, is finding which cheese best suits the situation. For crackers and cheese, you normally want a sharp or smoked cheddar since it can melt easily (if you choose to melt it), and also has a very strong, but simple, taste to it. For pizza, you typically will see people go towards a marble or a mozzarella cheese, and for good reason. However, the best use for cheese in my personal opinion, is on a charcuterie board, since you can use a wide variety of different cheeses and crackers, having a different type of cheese for each person's liking. One of the best items for a charcuterie board has to be some brie cheese with a form of sweet jelly on a cracker, since it has the creaminess from the brie, the salt from the cracter, and sweetness from the jelly, all coming together into a delicious snack that you end up eating way too much of. In the end, cheese is undoubtably the best food since it is so versatile and comes in so many forms and types, and every type seems to taste so good.
