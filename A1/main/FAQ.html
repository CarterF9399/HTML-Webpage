Frequently Asked Questions


What is the best cheese?

In my opinion, the best cheese is a nice, smoked Gouda cheese. It is so creamy and is so versatile, but I also have started to like Brie cheese a lot recently, too.

What is the worst cheese?

Personally, I do not like Burrata cheese. It is too weird of a texture for me and the thought of it being basically just a ball of cream is fairly offputting.

What is the most popular cheese?

Cheddar is the most popular cheese in the world, but it is followed closely by Mozzarella, which is the most popular cheese in the United States.

Why do some cheeses smell so bad?

Typically, the bad smell is due to whatever microorganisms are in the cheese and how long they have been there. Organisms like bacteria can create a very strong smell, but it just indicates that it has been aged for a long time.
